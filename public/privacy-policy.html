Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026. This policy describes how Caninelogy (owned and operated by Jim Justin M. Poso, United Arab Emirates) handles information when you use the K9 Management System at caninelogy.online.

Information we use

The service processes your account name, email address, sign-in credentials, workspace membership and the records you or your team enter. Billing identifiers and subscription status are used to manage paid access.

Why it is needed

This information supports sign-in, workspace permissions, record management and subscription access. Records are available to authorised workspace members according to their roles. Avoid entering unnecessary personal or sensitive information.

Service providers and storage

Cloudflare hosts the application and database. For Paddle purchases, Paddle processes payment information under its own privacy notice. The app uses a session cookie for sign-in and browser storage for preferences and local functionality.

Managing your information

Your workspace owner manages team access and workspace records. Contact your workspace owner about corrections or removal of records. Payment-data enquiries can be directed to Paddle. Removing browser storage does not delete server-held records.

Contact

For privacy enquiries contact jimjustinmposo@gmail.com.

This policy should be read alongside the Paddle Privacy Notice for payment-related data.
