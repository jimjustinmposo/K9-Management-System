## 1. Terms and Conditions — draft

### Operator and contact
Caninelogy’s K9 Management System is operated by Jim Justin M. Poso in the United Arab Emirates. The product website is caninelogy.online. Contact jimjustinmposo@gmail.com for product and account support.

### The service
Caninelogy is web-based software for managing K9 profiles, training, handlers, boarding, feeding, weights, medical records, grooming, inventory and related operational records. It includes role-based owner/member access and owner reporting. It is an administrative record-keeping tool, not veterinary advice, a diagnosis service or a guarantee of an animal’s health, suitability or operational readiness. Users remain responsible for professional decisions and checking their records and reports.

### Accounts and workspace responsibilities
Provide accurate account information and protect your sign-in credentials. Workspace owners manage member access and should grant access only to authorised people. Permissions vary by role and subscription status; having an account does not grant every action. Do not access another workspace without permission, misuse the service, interfere with its security or upload unlawful material.

### Customer records
Only enter records and personal information you are authorised to manage. You are responsible for the accuracy of your entries and for any notices or permissions needed when adding information about handlers, team members or boarding customers. Avoid unnecessary sensitive personal information in notes and uploads. Use available reports to retain copies needed for your operations; reports are not a complete database backup or guaranteed recovery service.

### Plans and payment
The displayed plans are USD 41 per month and USD 443 per year. Taxes are calculated at checkout. Plans include owner and member access and technical support. The application currently permits up to five active member accounts per workspace, in addition to the owner; unexpired pending invitations reserve member seats; this limit must match the plan presented before purchase.

Where a purchase is processed through Paddle, Paddle acts as the authorised reseller and its buyer terms apply to that purchase. Review the billing interval, taxes and total before confirming payment. Recurring subscriptions renew according to the terms shown at checkout until cancelled. Cancelling renewal and requesting a refund are separate actions.

### Subscription management
An authorised owner can use the subscription page to open billing management when a Paddle customer is linked. Paddle’s receipt and Buyer Support also provide assistance with purchases and subscriptions. Do not assume that signing out, removing a member, deleting a record or clearing browser storage cancels billing. Subscription cancellation does not itself delete workspace records.

### Support and availability
Technical support is available through jimjustinmposo@gmail.com and the website’s Contact Support page. No particular response time, uninterrupted availability, scheduled backup service or restoration guarantee is included in this draft. Internet, browser and provider availability can affect access. The service does not replace your own operational contingency arrangements.

### Other policies and rights
The Privacy Policy describes information processing. The Refund Policy explains how to request review of a payment. Nothing in these terms is intended to remove rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.

## 2. Privacy Policy — draft

### Who operates the service
Caninelogy is operated by Jim Justin M. Poso in the United Arab Emirates. For assistance contacting the operator about personal information, email jimjustinmposo@gmail.com. Your workspace owner also controls who is invited and what operational information is entered into that workspace.

### Information processed
- Account and workspace information: names, email addresses, workspace names, roles, membership status and account timestamps.
- Authentication information: password hashes and salts, session identifiers and expiry information, and reset or invitation information where those functions are used. Do not send your password to support.
- Operational records: K9 profiles and images, training and care history, boarding details, feeding, weight, medical and other records entered by your workspace. Records may contain personal information, including handler names, roles, email addresses and phone numbers, boarding-owner names and phone numbers, and information entered in free-text notes.
- Billing information: Paddle customer, subscription, price and transaction references, billing interval, status, relevant dates and webhook-processing records. The application’s reviewed billing routes use Paddle checkout rather than collecting card numbers directly.
- Support information: information you choose to include in email or WhatsApp messages.

### Why information is used
Information is used to create accounts, authenticate users, apply workspace permissions, maintain and display operational records, generate reports, administer subscription access and respond to support requests. Workspace owners and members can see or change information according to the permissions available to them. Owners are responsible for deciding what personal information should be entered about other people.

### Service providers and sharing
Cloudflare provides application hosting and the database infrastructure. Paddle handles purchases made through its checkout and associated payment support under its own terms and privacy notice. The application includes transactional-email integration with Resend; when enabled and used, recipient email addresses and message content are sent to that provider. Its current production enablement must be verified before finalising this disclosure.

If you choose WhatsApp support, the website opens WhatsApp with your message prepared; you choose whether to send it. WhatsApp processes information under its own policies. Email support is received at the operator’s Gmail address. Do not include passwords, API keys or unnecessary confidential records in support messages.

### Cookies, browser storage and local copies
The application uses a sign-in cookie named sentinel_session with a configured lifetime of up to 30 days. It is marked Secure, HttpOnly and SameSite=Lax. Session expiry is not the same as deletion of all related database records.

Browser storage remembers preferences such as the selected theme. Some operational modules also support local browser storage when their server backend is unavailable. Browser-held records can remain on that device until removed. Clearing browser storage does not delete server-held records, and signing out should not be treated as secure erasure of local records. Use trusted devices and manage downloaded reports and local copies carefully. A short-lived in-memory API cache also supports navigation and is cleared on logout.

### Retention and deletion
Cancelling a subscription does not automatically erase workspace information. Archiving a member disables access but is different from deleting an account or its related records. Deleting particular records or members through authorised controls does not establish that every provider record, local copy or historical backup has also been erased.

Contact the operator and, where appropriate, your workspace owner about access, correction or deletion requests. Depending on applicable law, you may have additional rights concerning your personal information. Requests involving other workspace members must not disclose their information without proper authority.

**Editorial blocker:** approve and implement a category-specific retention schedule before publication. ### Security and processing locations
The reviewed application uses HTTPS, password hashing, session cookies and server-side permission checks. These controls do not guarantee absolute security. Do not assume all information is stored only in the UAE: production provider locations and international-transfer arrangements need confirmation.

No sale-of-data or child-eligibility claims have been inferred from source code alone.

This policy concerns Caninelogy software-subscription payments, not charges a workspace records for its own boarding, grooming or other services. Caninelogy is operated by Jim Justin M. Poso in the United Arab Emirates. Product support is available at jimjustinmposo@gmail.com.

### Paddle purchases
For purchases completed through Paddle, refund requests are subject to Paddle’s Refund Policy, its Buyer Terms and applicable consumer law. Statutory rights and any additional rights expressly offered for your purchase remain applicable. This draft does not create a universal money-back guarantee or waive mandatory rights.

### Requesting help
Use Paddle Buyer Support or the support route in your purchase receipt to request a refund or exercise an applicable withdrawal right. Include the purchase email, transaction reference, date and reason for your request. You can also contact Caninelogy support for product-related assistance. Never send passwords or full payment-card details.

### Cancellation and refunds
Cancelling future renewal is separate from requesting repayment of an earlier charge. A refund request does not by itself confirm cancellation of future renewals; check the subscription status through billing management or Paddle support. Refund eligibility, the amount and processing depend on the applicable purchase terms, rights and the decision on the request. No fixed processing deadline is promised here.

### Problems with a purchase
Contact support if you believe there was a duplicate or incorrect charge or the product was not supplied as described. Nothing in this policy limits remedies available under mandatory law.
